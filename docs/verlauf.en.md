| 11.10. | Anomaly | In the `esphome logs` client the event `gedrueckt` arrived ~0.13–0.21 s **after** the OFF of the binary sensor each time, never at the ON (example: ON 44.26 s, OFF 49.84 s, event 50.05 s). | 12.10. ~14:00 | Installation test PC817 | Board connected at the junction box (grey wire on the 5-way WAGO), chime rings normally. Practically nothing is detected: only sporadic hits (floor 1 and ground floor once each, floor 2 never), no permanent ON. Jens: the chime is a **battery chime**. The assumption "the transformer drives the chime" had **never been verified** until then. |
| 12.10. ~14:30–15:00 | Wrong measurement | First measurement series in the box: 0 V at rest, ~0.3 V when pressed; −0.6 V at rest. Conclusions were drawn from this ("reverse diode clamps the sensing circuit", then "0.6 V = sensing voltage of the chime"). **All these values were measured on DC**, AC was intended – meaningless on AC voltage. It only showed up in a cross-check **at a known reference**: transformer terminals 2–4 on AC = 8 V ✔. The conclusions drawn until then are void. Lesson: [einmessen.en.md 0](einmessen.en.md#0-check-meter-range-and-the-device-at-the-other-end-first), [fehlersuche.en.md no. 19](fehlersuche.en.md#19-readings-make-no-sense). |
| 12.10. ~15:00–16:35 | AC measurements, reconnecting | On AC: 5-way WAGO ↔ floor 1 wire 6.33 V at rest, 6.9 V when pressed (multimeter high-impedance); across the 1 kΩ 0 V when pressed → no current through the board. Grey wire successively on the 5-way WAGO, transformer terminals 2, 4 and 8 – several presses each, **0 hits**; the only hits were connection transients (several channels at once). Jens: the 5-way WAGO is transformer pole 4 (all button wires meet there) – so the WAGO and terminal-4 tests were the same. Against the WAGO the wire **rose** when pressed (7.7 → 8.3 V) instead of dropping to ~0 → the measured wire is not the one switched by the button. |
| 12.10. ~16:45 | **Cause found** | Photo of the floor 1 chime: electronic chime with a **9 V block battery**, loudspeaker, board, only 2 wires. DC at the chime terminals at rest **9 V**. → Each floor has its **own battery chime with a contact input**; the button only shorts the chime loop, the transformer is **not** in the chime circuit. The optocoupler therefore never gets current – that explains all failed attempts. Ground floor probably also 9 V (*unverified*), floor 2 a different chime with 3 × C cell = 4.5 V. Buttons without illumination. What the transformer is still used for is *unclear*. Grey wire disconnected, chimes work normally. |
| 12.10. ~17:05 | Plan 2N7000 | Print sheet `plaene/klingel-schaltplan-mosfet.pdf`: per floor plus wire → 1 MΩ → gate 2N7000, drain → D5/D6/D7 (internal pull-up), source + minus wires + G common; rest = LOW, press = HIGH. **Assumption (unmeasured):** separate plus wire per floor, minus sides can be joined. If floor 2 is below ~3.5 V, BC547 instead of 2N7000. |
| 12.10. ~17:15–17:40 | Parts | No MOSFETs/transistors in stock, but 1 MΩ available. Considered: a bag of 100 × 2N7000 and a resistor assortment; ordered instead: a **transistor set with 24 types** (contains 2N7000 and BC547 as fallback for floor 2), delivery 14.10. |
| 12.10. | Side finding | During the installation test an event arrived in HA, but `binary_sensor.klingel_*_gedruckt` never showed "on". **Unexplained.** |
| 13.10. | Cross-check and docs | Firmware local = ESPHome Builder (checked) = `firmware/klingel.yaml` in the repo (except the deliberately removed MAC address). **Firmware not yet switched to 2N7000.** Repo updated: status, MOSFET plan and measuring sheet in `plaene/`, measurement lesson in einmessen/fehlersuche. Open: measure polarity per floor, rebuild from 14.10. |
| 14.10. | DC measurement | Only DC at the chime; when ringing it drops to **0 V** at the chime → contact input confirmed (the button shorts the battery loop). Per floor: terminal 1 = chime wire, terminal 2 = return. Floor 1 ~9.5 V, ground floor **2 × AA ≈ 3 V** (2.7 V in the box), floor 2 initially without batteries (silent, fitted with 3 × C cells on 15.10.). |

| 20.10. | Delivery + rebuild | BC517 and 9.1 MΩ arrived, plan changed to 9.1 MΩ (colour bands white–brown–blue). Board rebuilt and connected. |
| 20.10. ~15:15 | First test ✗ | All three channels HIGH at idle, no event for 9 presses (with the old, inverted firmware "idle LOW" would have shown as permanently "pressed"). Once the ground-floor chime rang an extra time (unexplained). |
| 20.10. ~15:30–15:55 | Troubleshooting | Measuring sheet (`plaene/klingel-messblatt-bc517.pdf`): reference 3V3↔G = +3.3 V; Kl.1 against grey **+3.1 / +9.47 / +5.5 V** (polarity correct). ## Discarded variants

| Variant | Reason |
|---|---|
| Power from the bell transformer (rectifier, electrolytic capacitor, step-down) | Hager ST303 has only 8 VA; socket available in the distribution board |
| Tap directly at the transformer via diodes in the return line | junction box offers all floors in one place, without intervening in the distribution board |
| DIN-rail enclosure in the distribution board | tap no longer in the distribution board |
| D1 mini Pro with external antenna | no metal cabinet; only if RSSI at the installation site is poor |
| PC814 | not available at short notice |
| Powering the bench test via an 18650 shield (power-bank board) | no detection (load cut-off suspected) → bare cell or lab power supply |
| Bench test with 5 V from the D1 mini (suggestion from `einmessen.md`) | G on the D1 is occupied, isolation doorbell/ESP side would not be preserved → separate battery |
| **Optocoupler tap (PC817 + 1N4007 + 1 kΩ)** | 12.10.: the chimes are battery chimes with a contact input, the transformer is not in the chime circuit → the LED never gets current. Replacement: BC517 + 9.1 MΩ (since 20.10.) |
| 2N7000 + 1 MΩ | 14.10.: ground-floor loop only ~3 V, gate threshold too marginal → BC517 |
| ESP between button and chime (trigger the chime via PC817) or ESP32 ADC with bias divider | briefly considered on 12.10. – based on the wrong DC measurement, therefore void |
